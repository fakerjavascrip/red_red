CHARACTER PROFILE / 人物档案

关羽 · Guan Yu

忠义勇猛义薄云天红色脸谱

关羽，字云长，是东汉末年的重要将领，后为蜀汉政权奠基者之一。在《三国演义》及传统戏曲中，他以忠诚、信义、勇武的形象深入人心。

Guan Yu, courtesy name Yunchang, was a prominent general of the late Eastern Han period. In Romance of the Three Kingdoms and traditional opera, he is remembered for loyalty, righteousness, and courage.

红色脸谱象征忠义与刚正。配合浓眉、锐利的眼形和对称的面部纹样，塑造出关羽庄严威武的舞台形象。

The red opera mask symbolizes integrity, loyalty, and fortitude. Bold eyebrows, sharp eyes, and symmetrical patterns convey his commanding stage presence.

01 / CHARACTER STORY

壹 · 人物故事

关羽是三国故事中极具代表性的人物，与刘备、张飞的情义故事广为流传。他的形象既有武将的勇猛，也体现重诺守义的精神。

Guan Yu is a celebrated figure in Three Kingdoms narratives. His bond with Liu Bei and Zhang Fei is widely remembered, while his character embodies martial courage and steadfast honor.

在戏曲舞台上，关羽的长髯、青龙偃月刀及鲜明的红色脸谱，共同构成辨识度极高的视觉形象。

On the opera stage, his long beard, Green Dragon Crescent Blade, and vivid red facial makeup create an instantly recognizable silhouette.

02 / FACIAL MASK CULTURE

贰 · 红色脸谱文化

在传统戏曲脸谱的色彩象征中，红色常表现忠诚、正直与刚毅。关羽的红脸形象尤其具有代表性，传递出忠义勇猛、义薄云天的精神气质。

In traditional opera symbolism, red represents loyalty, integrity, and fortitude. Guan Yu's red face is a celebrated visual expression of righteousness and courage.

夸张的眉形、锋利的眼型与流畅的中轴线，使脸谱既庄重威严，又具备强烈的舞台识别性。

Exaggerated eyebrows, sharp eye shapes, and a flowing central axis give the mask its dignified, dramatic identity.

03 / HEBEI BANGZI OPERA

叁 · 河北梆子艺术

河北梆子是中国传统地方戏曲剧种， 以高亢激越的唱腔、鲜明的节奏和 富有感染力的舞台表演而著称。

Hebei Bangzi is a traditional regional Chinese opera known for its powerful vocal style, distinctive rhythms, and expressive stage performances.

本作品将戏曲人物、传统脸谱与 NFC 数字音频技术相结合， 让观众通过实体海报进入数字空间， 进一步了解人物故事与戏曲文化。

This project combines traditional opera characters, facial mask design, and NFC audio technology. It connects physical posters with digital experiences, allowing audiences to explore character stories and traditional opera culture.
